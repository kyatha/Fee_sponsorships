About PhD Fee Bridge

By graduate students and friends, for graduate students.

We're not rich. We're PhD students, recent graduates and members of the wider community. Many of us were once exactly where you are: an application ready to go, and a fee standing between us and the submit button. We dared to dream, and we know how unfair it feels when something this small gets in the way.

This is our small gift to you. It won't cover everything, but it can make sure the fee isn't what stops you.

Good luck with your application. We're cheering for you.

Kyatha and friends

How it works

  1. Apply. Students from African countries applying for a PhD or MS/PhD at UMass Amherst share a short research statement and verify their email.
  2. Sponsors step in. One sponsor covers the whole $90 fee, or several chip in and pool their shares. Nobody pays until a fee is fully pledged.
  3. The fee gets paid. Sponsors pay within 24 hours, the fee goes to UMass, and the applicant confirms once their application shows it as paid.

Where the money goes

Pool shares go into one account run by the organisers, and every dollar goes to UMass Amherst application fees. Nobody takes a cut. We keep a record of every pledge and payment, and any pledge that doesn't complete is released, so nothing is charged.

Your privacy

Sponsors see an applicant's name, country, program and research statement. Email addresses, UMass applicant IDs and application numbers are never shown publicly. The sponsor who pays a fee sees the application number and UMass ID so the payment reaches the right application. Sponsors can always stay anonymous.
